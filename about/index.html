Who We Are

Call-First, Flat-Priced Plumbing

Clearwater Express Plumbing is a licensed plumbing service for Clearwater, FL and the surrounding Pinellas County area. We work on a call-first model, no online booking forms, no chat widgets standing in for a real conversation, just a phone line answered by someone who can actually get a technician moving.

Every technician we dispatch is licensed under Florida's Department of Business & Professional Regulation, and every job, from a same-day water heater swap to a full softener install, gets a flat price agreed to before any work starts. We don't quote over the phone sight unseen and then change the number once a technician is standing in your kitchen.

We built the business around two simple ideas: answer the phone every time, and quote honestly before touching anything. Neither sounds complicated, but they're the two things people most often tell us other companies got wrong before they called us instead, and they're the two things we hold every technician to on every call, not just the ones being watched closely.

Our Approach To Pricing

Flat Quotes, Explained Before You Agree

We don't publish a fixed price list because the honest price for a repair depends on what a technician actually finds once they're on site, not a phone-guess. What we do instead is inspect first, explain what's wrong in plain language, and give you one flat number before anything is touched. If the scope changes once work is underway, that gets quoted separately and approved before we continue, never added to the bill afterward.

Licensing & Compliance

Florida-Licensed, Building-Code Compliant

Every technician on our team holds a Florida plumbing license through the state Department of Business & Professional Regulation, and every installation follows the Florida Building Code's plumbing standards, the same standards local building departments inspect against. We don't cut corners on permitting or code compliance to save time on a job.

Hurricane Season

Built For Gulf Coast Weather

Operating on Florida's Gulf Coast means planning around hurricane season as a normal part of the business, not an occasional disruption. We keep our dispatch line staffed through storms when it's safe to do so, and we treat sump pump checks and outdoor fixture prep as a real seasonal service, not an afterthought tacked onto a general plumbing visit.

Why Call-First

No Forms Standing Between You And Help

We didn't build an online booking system because a plumbing problem usually isn't something you want to describe in a dropdown menu. A phone call lets us actually hear what's going on, ask a follow-up question, and get a real sense of urgency, all things a web form can't do. That's the whole reason every page on this site leads back to a phone number instead of a contact form.

FAQs

Common Questions About Us

Do you use subcontractors, or your own technicians?

Every technician who shows up at your door is dispatched directly by us and licensed under Florida's Department of Business & Professional Regulation, not a third-party subcontractor.

How is the price actually decided?

A technician inspects the issue first, then gives you one flat number before touching anything. Nothing changes after work starts unless new scope is found and approved separately.

Do you serve areas outside your main service pages?

Often yes. Call and describe your address, we'll confirm coverage directly rather than relying only on the areas already listed, since real coverage tends to extend a bit past the exact town boundaries we've written pages for.

What happens if you find a bigger problem mid-job?

We stop, explain what we found, and quote the additional work separately before continuing, rather than folding a surprise cost into the original price.
