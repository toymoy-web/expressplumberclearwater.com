Toilet Repair & Installation

From A Running Toilet To A Full Replacement

Toilet Repair & Installation covers the full range of what actually goes wrong: a toilet that keeps running and quietly raising your water bill, a wobbling bowl from a failed wax ring, a fill valve that won't shut off, or a unit old enough that replacement makes more sense than another repair. We handle all of it in a single visit for most calls.

Why This Happens

Wear, Hard Water, and Age

Toilet components wear out from ordinary daily use, but Clearwater's hard water speeds that up. Mineral buildup stiffens flapper seals and fill valves faster than it would with softer water, which is a big part of why running toilets and weak flushes show up here more often than homeowners expect from a fixture with no moving parts they can see. Older toilets built before modern low-flow standards are also more prone to weak flushes as internal components age, independent of the hard water issue.

Common Signs

Signs You Need Repair Or Replacement

Toilet Runs Constantly

Water running long after a flush, usually a worn flapper or fill valve.

Rocking Or Wobbling

A bowl that shifts when you sit down, often a failed wax ring seal at the base.

Weak Or Incomplete Flush

Needing multiple flushes to clear the bowl.

Cracks Or Frequent Repairs

Hairline cracks in the tank or bowl, or repairing the same unit repeatedly.

Repair vs. Replace

Which Makes Sense For Your Toilet

Repair

  • Lower cost for an isolated issue
  • Faster for a single failed part
  • Good fit for newer units
  • Doesn't help a genuinely old unit

Full Replacement

  • Right call for repeated repairs
  • Better water efficiency on newer models
  • Fresh wax ring seal included
  • Higher upfront cost

FAQs

Common Questions

My toilet runs after every flush. Is that a cheap fix?

Usually yes, it's typically a worn flapper or fill valve, one of our most common and least expensive repairs.

Is a wobbling toilet urgent?

It's worth addressing soon. A failed wax ring seal can let water leak under the floor if left too long.

Do you install customer-supplied toilets?

Yes, we can install a unit you've already purchased, or supply one ourselves.

How much water does a running toilet actually waste?

It varies by leak size, but a constantly running toilet is one of the most common causes of an unexplained water bill spike.
