Privacy Policy

How Clearwater Express Plumbing handles information collected when you contact us.

Clearwater Express Plumbing ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you contact us by phone or email, and how we use it.

Information We Collect

What We Collect

When you call or email us, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, service address, and details about the plumbing issue you're contacting us about. We do not use online booking forms, so information is collected only through direct phone or email contact.

How We Use It

How Information Is Used

We use the information you provide to schedule and complete service, communicate with you about your appointment, and maintain accurate business records. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.
