Leak Detection

When You Know Something's Wrong But Not What

Leak Detection is our single most-called service in Clearwater, and most people who call don't yet know where the leak actually is. They've noticed a water bill that jumped for no clear reason, a meter that keeps turning with every fixture in the house shut off, or a damp spot that showed up with no obvious source. That uncertainty is exactly what we're set up to resolve.

Why This Happens Here

Sandy Soil and a High Water Table

Much of Clearwater sits on loose, sandy soil with a high water table close to the surface. When an underground line develops even a small leak, the sandy soil around it washes away quickly, which can hide the leak's actual location from a surface inspection while still letting real damage develop underneath. That's a genuine local condition, not something true everywhere in Florida.

Common Signs

Signs You May Have a Hidden Leak

Unexplained High Water Bill

A bill that jumps with no change in household water use.

Meter Still Running

Your water meter keeps turning even with every fixture in the house off.

Warm Spot On The Floor

A warm patch on tile or flooring, often a sign of a hot water line leak under the slab.

Damp Spots Or Soft Ground

Unexplained wet patches indoors, or soggy ground outside with no rain.

Before You Call

You Don't Need To Know Where It Is First

Most callers describe the same thing: the meter is running with the water off, or the bill went up and there's no obvious reason why. You don't need to pinpoint the leak yourself before calling. Diagnosing exactly where it is, and how it's getting found, is the actual job, not something we expect you to already know.

Spot Repair vs. Line Replacement

Once The Leak Is Found

Spot Repair

  • Lower cost for a single, isolated leak
  • Faster turnaround
  • Best when the rest of the line tests sound
  • May not address widespread old pipe

Line Replacement

  • Right call for older, failing pipe runs
  • Prevents repeat leaks nearby
  • Longer-term fix
  • Higher upfront cost

FAQs

Common Questions

My water bill jumped but I don't see any water. Could it still be a leak?

Yes, that's actually the most common way a hidden leak shows up here, especially with a slab or underground line.

Do you have to dig up my yard to find it?

Not usually. Acoustic and pressure-testing methods locate most leaks without digging until the actual repair.

Can you find a leak under a concrete slab?

Yes, slab leak detection is one of our most common calls given how much of Clearwater's housing sits on a slab foundation.
