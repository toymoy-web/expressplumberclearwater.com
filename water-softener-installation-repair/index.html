Why This Happens Here

Clearwater's Hard Water Problem

Florida's Gulf Coast aquifers carry high mineral content, mostly calcium and magnesium, which is what makes water "hard." That mineral load doesn't go away on its own. It builds up inside water heaters, clogs faucet aerators and shower heads, leaves spotting on dishes and glassware, and shortens the working life of nearly every appliance that touches water in the house. A softener is the only real fix, not a workaround.

Common Signs

Signs You Need a Softener Looked At

White Scale Buildup

Crusty white deposits around faucets, shower heads, and the coffee maker.

Dry Skin & Hair

Soap doesn't rinse clean, leaving a filmy residue on skin and in hair.

Spotty Dishes & Glassware

Cloudy spots on glassware even straight out of the dishwasher.

Existing System Not Working

Salt tank not dropping, water tasting different, or the unit cycling constantly.

Salt-Based vs. Salt-Free

Which System Fits Your Home

Salt-Free Conditioner

  • Lower ongoing maintenance
  • No salt bags to carry or store
  • Conditions minerals rather than removing them
  • Better fit for softener-sensitive plumbing

Salt-Based Softener

  • Actually removes hardness minerals
  • Best result for severe Gulf Coast hard water
  • Well-understood, widely serviced technology
  • Needs periodic salt refills

FAQs

Common Questions

Do I need a softener if I'm on city water, not a well?

Yes. Pinellas County's municipal water supply is drawn from the same hard Florida aquifers, so city water here is hard too, not just well water.

Can you repair a softener you didn't install?

Yes. We service existing systems from other installers as long as parts are still available for that model.

How long does a typical install take?

Most whole-home installs are completed in a single visit, usually a few hours depending on your existing plumbing layout.

Will a softener help my water heater last longer?

Yes. Softened water dramatically slows the scale buildup that shortens tank life, so pairing the two is common here.
