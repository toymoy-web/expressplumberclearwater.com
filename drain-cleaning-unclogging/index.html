Our line runs 24/7, because a backed-up drain rarely waits for business hours, and most calls are resolved the same day with either snaking or hydro jetting.

Why This Happens Here

Root Intrusion and Older Pipe

Many Clearwater neighborhoods have mature landscaping with root systems that find their way into older clay or cast iron sewer laterals over time, which is a real and common cause of recurring backups here. Older homes also carry decades of grease and soap buildup in the same pipe runs, which narrows the line gradually until a clog finally sticks. Kitchen lines in particular tend to build up faster in older homes that were plumbed before modern grease-trap expectations became standard.

Common Signs

Signs Your Drain Needs Attention

Slow Draining

Sinks, tubs, or showers that empty slower than they used to.

Gurgling Sounds

Air bubbling back up through a drain, usually a sign of a partial blockage further down.

Recurring Clogs

The same drain clogging again a few weeks after it was last cleared.

Sewage Odor

A persistent bad smell near a drain, sometimes a sign of a blockage further down the line.

Snaking vs. Hydro Jetting

Which One Your Drain Needs

Cable Snaking

  • Faster for a single, simple clog
  • Lower cost for straightforward jobs
  • Good first step on new drains
  • May not fully clear root intrusion

Hydro Jetting

  • Scours the full pipe wall clean
  • Clears root intrusion and grease buildup
  • Longer-lasting result
  • Recommended for recurring clogs

FAQs

Common Questions

Can you come out tonight?

Yes, our emergency line runs 24/7 including nights, weekends, and holidays.

Will snaking damage my pipes?

No, when done correctly cable snaking is safe for standard residential drain lines.

Why does the same drain keep clogging?

Recurring clogs usually mean root intrusion or heavy buildup that only hydro jetting fully clears.

Can tree roots really get into a sewer line?

Yes, it's a genuinely common issue in older Clearwater neighborhoods with mature trees planted near the sewer lateral line.
