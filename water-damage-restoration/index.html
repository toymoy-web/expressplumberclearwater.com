Water Damage Restoration

Cleanup Starts With A Dedicated Team

Water Damage Restoration is a different job from fixing the pipe that caused it. Once a leak, storm, or hurricane has actually damaged flooring, drywall, or a subfloor, that cleanup and structural drying is handled by a dedicated restoration line and technician, not the same crew dispatched for a routine plumbing repair. If you need the leak itself repaired rather than the aftermath, see our Leak Detection or Drain Cleaning pages instead.

Why This Happens Here

Hurricane Season and Storm Surge Risk

The City of Clearwater's own Building Department publishes hurricane-season guidance every year covering securing outdoor plumbing fixtures and checking sump pumps ahead of a storm. That guidance exists because it reflects a real, recurring local risk, not a generic Florida checklist. Beyond named storms, a burst pipe or failed water heater can cause the same kind of damage on any ordinary day, and Pinellas County's high water table means water that gets into a structure has fewer places to drain away on its own than it would further inland.

Common Signs

Signs You Need Restoration, Not Just A Repair

Standing Water

Visible water pooling on flooring after a leak, appliance failure, or storm.

Warped Or Soft Flooring

Flooring that feels spongy or has visibly warped near a water source.

Water-Stained Drywall Or Ceiling

Discoloration or bubbling paint from water intrusion.

Musty Odor

A persistent damp smell, often a sign moisture is still trapped somewhere.

Before We Arrive

What To Do Before We Arrive

Shut Off Water If Safe

If the source is a visible leak and the shutoff is reachable without standing in water, turn it off before we arrive.

Stay Away From Outlets

Do not touch outlets, switches, or plugged-in devices anywhere near standing water.

Don't Use Damaged Electronics

Leave any electronics that got wet unplugged and untouched until they're checked.

Move Valuables Clear

Shift furniture, rugs, and belongings out of the affected area if you can do so safely.

Our Process

How Restoration Works

Drying is the part that actually prevents mold and long-term structural problems, not the initial cleanup. We measure moisture rather than assume a surface looks dry means it is.

01

Assess

Full inspection to confirm the extent of the water intrusion.

02

Extract

Standing water removed and affected materials identified.

03

Dry

Industrial drying equipment placed to fully dry the structure, not just the surface.

04

Verify

Moisture readings confirmed dry before equipment is removed.

DIY Cleanup vs. Professional Restoration

Why Drying Matters More Than Mopping

DIY Cleanup

  • Removes visible surface water
  • No moisture verification
  • Trapped moisture often missed
  • Mold risk if not fully dried

Professional Restoration

  • Industrial drying equipment
  • Moisture levels actually measured
  • Structural drying, not just surface
  • Documented for insurance if needed

FAQs

Common Questions

Is this the same line as your regular plumbing number?

No, water damage restoration runs on its own dedicated line so it reaches a technician who specializes in cleanup and drying, not general plumbing repair.

Do you also fix the pipe that caused the damage?

That repair is handled through our standard plumbing line. Restoration handles the cleanup and drying once the source is addressed.

Can you document damage for an insurance claim?

Yes, our assessment includes documentation of the affected areas and moisture readings.

How do I know if drywall needs to be cut out or can just dry out?

That depends on how long it was wet and the moisture reading we take. We'll show you the reading and explain the recommendation before any material is removed.
