Preparing Your Plumbing for a Florida Cold Snap

It doesn't happen often here, which is exactly why homes aren't built to handle it well when it does.

Written by the Clearwater Express Plumbing Team · September 16, 2026

DISPATCH LINE

Intro

The Rare Exception Worth Preparing For

Clearwater rarely sees freezing temperatures, but the occasional cold snap does happen, and Florida homes are built for a climate that almost never freezes, which means exposed pipe here is more vulnerable during a rare freeze than the same pipe would be in a climate built to expect it.

Why It's Different Here

Homes Aren't Built For This

Homes in consistently cold climates run water lines deep in insulated walls and below frost lines by design. Florida homes, built for a climate that rarely freezes, often have supply lines running through uninsulated exterior walls, garages, or exposed outdoor locations, which is fine almost every year and a real risk during the rare hard freeze.

Outdoor Fixtures First

The Most Vulnerable Points

Hose bibs, irrigation backflow preventers, and any exposed outdoor piping are the first things at risk during a freeze warning. Disconnecting hoses, covering exposed spigots with an insulated cover, and letting exterior faucets drip slightly during a hard freeze warning are all real, effective steps.

Inside The Home

Cabinet Doors And Faucet Drips

For pipes running through exterior walls, particularly under kitchen and bathroom sinks, opening cabinet doors during a freeze warning lets warmer indoor air reach the pipe. A slow drip from an affected faucet keeps water moving, which meaningfully reduces freeze risk compared to fully static water sitting in a pipe.

If A Pipe Does Freeze

What To Do Before It Bursts

If a pipe is frozen but hasn't burst, shutting off the main water supply and gently warming the affected section, never with an open flame, gives the best chance of avoiding a burst. If water is already leaking or spraying, shut off the main immediately and call for emergency service rather than waiting.

After The Cold Passes

Check Before You Assume Everything's Fine

Once temperatures rise, check exposed pipes for cracks even if nothing burst outright, since a hairline crack from freezing can leak slowly for days before becoming obvious. It's worth a quick visual check rather than assuming a freeze passed without any effect.

Irrigation Systems In A Freeze

Backflow Preventers Need Attention Too

Above-ground backflow preventers on irrigation systems are particularly vulnerable during a hard freeze, since they're specifically designed to sit exposed for inspection access. Wrapping or insulating an exposed backflow preventer during a freeze warning is a small step that can prevent a costly repair.

Pool Equipment

Another Commonly Forgotten Risk

Pool pumps, filters, and exposed pool plumbing are also vulnerable during a rare freeze, since they're built for a climate that almost never requires freeze protection. Running the pump continuously during a hard freeze warning keeps water moving through the system, which meaningfully reduces the risk of a cracked pump housing or filter.

Mobile Homes And Manufactured Housing

A Higher-Risk Category Worth Naming

Mobile and manufactured homes often have supply lines running through unenclosed underbelly space with minimal insulation, making them meaningfully more vulnerable during a freeze than a standard slab-built home. If you live in one, freeze prep is worth taking more seriously than the general advice above suggests for typical construction.

Community Resources

Where To Check For Official Freeze Warnings

The National Weather Service issues freeze warnings with enough lead time to complete basic prep in most cases. Signing up for local weather alerts is a simple way to make sure you're not caught off guard by a freeze warning issued while you're not actively checking the forecast.

Vacant Homes During A Freeze

A Special Case Worth Planning For

If you travel during winter months, a vacant home during a freeze is at higher risk since no one is there to notice early warning signs like a dripping faucet left unattended or a cabinet door left open. Asking a neighbor or property manager to check on the home during a hard freeze warning is worth arranging in advance.

A Quick Recap

The Short Version If You're Skimming

Florida homes are built for a climate that rarely freezes, which makes exposed pipe more vulnerable during the rare cold snap than the same pipe would be in a colder climate. Disconnecting hoses, opening cabinet doors, and letting faucets drip during a hard freeze warning are the highest-value, lowest-effort steps.

If You're Traveling During A Freeze Warning

What To Arrange Before You Leave

If a freeze warning is issued while you're out of town, having someone you trust check the thermostat setting and confirm heat is running, and glance at exposed outdoor fixtures, is worth arranging ahead of time rather than hoping for the best from a distance.

FAQs

Common Questions

How cold does it actually need to get to be a risk here?

Sustained temperatures at or below freezing for several hours put exposed pipe genuinely at risk, even if it's a rare event for this area.

Is pipe insulation worth installing for such a rare event?

For exposed outdoor pipe and vulnerable exterior-wall runs, yes, it's inexpensive relative to the cost of a burst pipe and resulting water damage.

Should I run my pool pump during a freeze warning even overnight?

Yes, keeping water moving through pool equipment during a hard freeze meaningfully lowers the risk of cracked housings or filters.

Is a mobile home really more at risk during a cold snap?

Yes, generally. Underbelly plumbing on mobile and manufactured homes is often less insulated than a standard slab-built home's supply lines.

How much notice do freeze warnings usually give?

Typically at least a day, sometimes more, which is generally enough time to complete the basic prep steps described here.

Should I worry about pipes freezing overnight even without an official warning?

If overnight lows are forecast near or below freezing for several hours, it's worth taking basic precautions even without a formal warning issued.

Does insulating just the most exposed section of pipe help meaningfully?

Yes, targeted insulation on the most exposed sections captures most of the benefit without needing to insulate every foot of exterior pipe.

Should I be more worried about a freeze at night or during the day?

Overnight is the higher-risk window, since temperatures typically bottom out well after sunset and before sunrise.

Is there a risk to my plumbing from AC systems working harder in a cold snap?

Not directly to plumbing, though it's a good reminder that a home's systems all get stressed a bit differently during unusual weather.
