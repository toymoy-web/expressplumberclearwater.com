The Area

Plumbing In North Greenwood

North Greenwood is a historically African-American neighborhood in Clearwater, just north of downtown. Its ZIP code is 33755. The City of Clearwater created the North Greenwood Community Redevelopment Area, which covers about 841 acres, and Pinellas County approved it in May 2023. The Martin Luther King Jr. Avenue corridor is described as the historic center of the community.

The North Greenwood Recreation and Aquatic Complex, Cherry Harris Park, and the Pinellas Trail are all in or beside the neighborhood, and it is within easy reach of downtown.

Housing

Smaller Homes, Long Histories

One housing guide counts about 497 homes with a median year built of 1953. It describes modest single-family homes, including cottages and ranch-style bungalows, with an average size of about 1,500 square feet on a median lot of roughly 5,400 square feet. These are approximate aggregator figures.

Smaller older homes often have a bathroom, a kitchen, and a laundry area close together, so one failed part can affect several fixtures. That is general plumbing knowledge. We keep repairs practical, explain what we find, and give you the choices without pressure.

City Resources

Housing Repair Programs

The City of Clearwater publishes its own housing programs, including home preservation resources. Eligibility and funding rules are set by the city, not by us, and they apply across the city rather than to one neighborhood. If you are thinking about a larger repair, it is worth checking the city's current program pages first.

FAQs

Questions From North Greenwood

Where is North Greenwood?

It lies just north of downtown Clearwater, and the housing guide we used lists ZIP code 33755. Martin Luther King Jr. Avenue is the historic center of the community.

Do you work on older homes here?

Yes. We start by finding out what is in the house and then explain the options, including repair before replacement when it makes sense.

Does the city offer help with home repairs?

The City of Clearwater publishes housing and home preservation programs. Eligibility is set by the city, so check its current pages or contact the city directly.

Do you cover the whole neighborhood?

Yes, and call with your address if you are near the edge and we will confirm coverage.
