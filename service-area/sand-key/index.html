The Area

Plumbing On Sand Key

Sand Key is a barrier island between the Gulf of Mexico and the Intracoastal Waterway, south of Clearwater Beach. The island is long and several towns share it, so only the northern end falls inside the city of Clearwater. If you are not sure which side of a town line your building is on, call and we will check the address with you.

One housing guide lists Sand Key under ZIP code 33767 and counts about 1,908 homes with a median year built of 1982. It says condominiums dominate, with most residents living in towers along Gulf Boulevard, plus two small communities with townhouses and detached homes and one section of single-family homes in a private community. These are approximate figures from an aggregator.

Condo Living

When One Leak Reaches Several Units

In a tower, plumbing is shared in ways a house never is. Supply risers and drain stacks can run past many units, so a leak or backup in one place can show up as a stain or a slow drain somewhere else. This is general building knowledge and not a claim about any specific tower.

Because of that, the first useful question is often whether the problem is inside your unit or in a shared line. Your building manager or association can usually answer that. Some buildings also have their own rules for contractor access and insurance, so it helps to ask before we arrive.

What We Look At

Practical Plumbing Notes

Inside The Unit

Water heaters, toilets, faucets, and the drains under your own sinks are usually yours to repair, and we handle them directly.

Shared Lines

A problem in a riser or stack involves the building. We can document what we find so you can pass it to your manager.

Access Rules

Many associations set hours or paperwork for contractors. Ask your office early so a visit is not delayed.

Know Your Shutoffs

Find your unit shutoff and learn whether the building has one for your floor. Both save time during a leak.

FAQs

Questions From Sand Key

Do you work on condo towers along Gulf Boulevard?

Yes. We handle plumbing inside the unit and can document findings on shared lines for your building manager.

Is all of Sand Key in the city of Clearwater?

No. Several towns share the island, and only the northern end is in Clearwater. Call with your address and we will confirm whether we cover it.

What if the leak might be coming from the unit above me?

Tell your building manager right away and call us. We can look at your side and describe what we find, and the building handles any shared line.
