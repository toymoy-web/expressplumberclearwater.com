City Overview

Plumbing In Largo, Florida

Largo is Pinellas County's third-largest city, with roughly 82,000 residents spread across six ZIP codes in the 33770s. First incorporated in 1905, Largo stayed a small agricultural town until a population boom in the 1950s through 1970s turned it into one of the county's biggest bedroom communities. That growth era is the single biggest factor in the plumbing calls we get here today. Largo also runs its own wastewater reclamation system even though its drinking water is supplied by Pinellas County, and our emergency dispatch is available around the clock across all six of those ZIP codes.

Growth Era & Pipe Material

A City Built In One Fast Stretch

Because so much of Largo was built in a single 20-to-30-year boom, cast-iron sewer lines and galvanized steel supply pipe from that era are still common across the city, especially in the older neighborhoods around Largo Central Park and Ridgecrest. Newer pockets of construction exist, but the postwar-era plumbing is the dominant story here, not the exception, which is different from a city like Belleair where the housing stock skews much older and more custom.

A Note On The Growth

From Farm Town To County's Third-Largest City

Largo was an agricultural exporter until the 1960s, when rapid population growth turned it into a bedroom community almost overnight, and the city switched back and forth between town and city status before settling permanently as a city in 1974. Largo Central Park, which opened in 1995 on the site of the old Pinellas County Fairgrounds, sits on some of that same transitional land. The speed of that growth is exactly why so much of the city's underground plumbing dates to the same narrow window of construction.

FAQs

Questions From Largo

Why do so many Largo homes have cast-iron sewer lines?

Largo's population boomed in the 1950s through 70s, and cast iron and galvanized pipe were standard for that era of construction across the city.

Do you cover all of Largo's ZIP codes?

Yes, all six: 33770, 33771, 33773, 33774, 33778, and 33779, from Ridgecrest and Largo Central Park out to the newer construction near the city limits.

Is Largo's water different from Clearwater's?

No, Largo's drinking water comes from the same Pinellas County regional supply as Clearwater, though Largo runs its own separate wastewater reclamation system.

Can you handle a repipe for a whole postwar-era house?

Yes, full or partial repiping is a common request in Largo's older neighborhoods, and we'll walk you through the real options before starting.

Is emergency service available in Largo?

Yes, our dispatch line is answered 24 hours a day, every day, for burst pipes, active leaks, and no-hot-water calls across all six Largo ZIP codes.
