The Area

Plumbing On Clearwater Beach

Clearwater Beach is a barrier island neighborhood of the city of Clearwater. The Clearwater Memorial Causeway connects it to downtown, and its ZIP code is 33767. Pier 60 and its nightly sunset festival sit near the center of the island, with McKay Park and Pier 60 Park among the parks listed closest to it.

One housing guide counts about 2,076 homes in the neighborhood and gives a median year built of 1974. It describes a mix of condo buildings, ranch-style homes, coastal contemporary and Key West style houses, and waterfront estates. These are approximate aggregator figures, but the mix is clear enough to matter, because a condo tower, a 1970s ranch house, and a new build each call for a different approach.

Coastal Conditions

Salt Air, Flood Zones, And Storm Season

The same guide notes that Clearwater Beach sits in a flood zone, and Gulf storms can push surge onto the island. After any flooding, a water heater, shutoff valve, or electrical panel that sat in water should be inspected before it is turned back on. If that sounds like your situation, call us and tell us what got wet.

Salt air is also hard on exposed metal. Outdoor showers, hose bibs, and exterior shutoff valves face it every day. This is general coastal plumbing knowledge and not a finding about any one street, but it is why we check for stiff valves and corrosion early instead of waiting for a failure.

What We Look At

Practical Plumbing Notes

Condo And Building Coordination

Many beach condos share supply risers and drain stacks, so a leak can reach neighbors. We coordinate with property managers when a problem may involve a shared line.

Exposed Outdoor Fittings

Outdoor showers, hose bibs, and exterior shutoffs sit in salt air. We look for corrosion and stiff valves before they fail.

Find Your Main Shutoff

Locate your main shutoff before you need it. In a condo it may be a unit valve, a building valve, or both.

Check After A Storm

Water heaters, supply lines, and drain connections near the floor deserve a look after any flooding.

FAQs

Questions From Clearwater Beach

Do you serve Clearwater Beach condos as well as houses?

Yes. We work on both. For a condo it helps to know whether the problem is inside your unit or in a shared line, and your building manager can usually tell you which.

Is Clearwater Beach part of the city of Clearwater?

Yes. It is a neighborhood of the city, connected to downtown by the Clearwater Memorial Causeway.

What should I do after storm flooding on the island?

Close the main shutoff if water is still entering the home, stay away from wet electrical equipment, and call us. A water heater that sat in floodwater should be checked before it runs again.

Which ZIP code covers Clearwater Beach?

The ZIP code listed for the beach neighborhood is 33767. If you are unsure whether your address is inside the city, call and we will confirm coverage.
