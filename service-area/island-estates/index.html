The Area

Plumbing In Island Estates

Island Estates sits between downtown Clearwater and Clearwater Beach, along the Intracoastal Waterway, and uses ZIP code 33767. Sunset Sam Park at Island Estates, McKay Park, and Pier 60 Park are among the parks listed nearby.

One housing guide counts about 1,609 homes with a median year built of 1975, and says most were built in the 1960s and 1970s. It describes ranch-style homes with stucco walls, condominium towers, modern estates, and Mediterranean-inspired villas. These are approximate figures from an aggregator.

Housing

Larger Homes, Buildings From Different Eras

The same guide puts the average single-family home at about 3,020 square feet, well above the national average it cites. A house that size usually has more bathrooms and fixtures than a small one, which means more places for a supply valve or drain to fail and more demand on the water heater. That is general plumbing knowledge and not a finding about any one home.

The guide also profiles condo buildings completed in 1973, 1988, and 2007. Plumbing in a 1973 building and a 2007 building is not the same job, so we ask about the building and the unit before we arrive.

What We Look At

Practical Plumbing Notes

Water Heater Capacity

A larger home with several bathrooms can outgrow a small tank. We size a replacement to how your household actually uses hot water.

Condo Buildings

Shared risers and drain stacks can carry a problem from one unit to another. We coordinate with building management when a shared line may be involved.

Lots Near The Water

Properties close to canals and the waterway can stay damp. We watch outdoor drains, irrigation lines, and pool equipment plumbing for slow leaks.

Before Storm Season

Know where your main shutoff is and make sure it turns. A stuck valve is a bad surprise during an active leak.

Water Heater Acting Up?

Call and tell us about your home and your hot water use, and we will talk through repair or replacement.

FAQs

Questions From Island Estates

Do you service condo buildings in Island Estates?

Yes. Tell us the building and unit number when you call, and let us know if the problem could be in a shared line so we can involve your property manager if needed.

Are Island Estates and Clearwater Beach the same area?

They are separate neighborhoods. Island Estates lies between downtown and the beach, and the housing guide we used lists both under ZIP code 33767.

Can you size a water heater for a larger home?

Yes. We ask how many bathrooms you have and how many people live in the home, then recommend a unit that fits the real demand.

Which ZIP code is Island Estates in?

33767 is the ZIP code listed for the neighborhood.
