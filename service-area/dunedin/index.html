City Overview

Plumbing In Dunedin, Florida

Dunedin is a Gulf Coast city of roughly 36,000 residents on Pinellas County's west side, bordered by Clearwater to the south and the Gulf of Mexico to the west. Unlike Clearwater and most of its neighbors, Dunedin runs its own municipal water utility, pumping groundwater from local wells in the Floridan aquifer rather than buying treated water from Pinellas County. That gives Dunedin homes a slightly different water chemistry profile than county-supplied neighborhoods nearby, and our emergency dispatch is available around the clock for whatever that water chemistry eventually causes.

Housing & Water

Bungalows, Wells, and Hard Water

Downtown Dunedin's housing stock leans older, with bungalows built in the 1920s through 1940s lining the streets around Main Street and the Pinellas Trail, which runs on the old Orange Belt Railway bed straight through the middle of town. Newer subdivisions further from downtown, like Highland Woods, sit on more recent construction. Because the city draws its own well water, hard-water mineral buildup is a genuine, ongoing issue across both the historic core and the newer neighborhoods alike.

A Note On The Name

Scottish Roots, Real Preservation

Dunedin takes its name from Dun Eideann, the Scottish Gaelic name for Edinburgh, and the city still keeps that heritage alive through the annual Highland Games rather than treating it as tourist branding. The downtown business district has intentionally avoided large commercial signage and chain retail for decades, which is part of why so much of the original 1920s-40s housing stock, and its original plumbing, is still standing rather than torn down for redevelopment.

Neighborhoods

Areas We Cover In Dunedin

We regularly work in the established residential streets of Wilshire, Winchester, and Virginia Park just outside downtown, as well as the more suburban Highland Woods and the golf-course-adjacent Fairway Estates further from the core. Each of those areas skews a different age, so we don't assume one pipe material or fixture era across the whole city.

If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak In Dunedin

Shut Off The Main Valve

Locate and close Dunedin's main shutoff, usually near the street-side hose bib, before water spreads further.

Kill Power To Wet Areas
