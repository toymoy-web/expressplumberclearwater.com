City Overview

Plumbing In Safety Harbor, Florida

Safety Harbor is a small city of about 17,000 people on the west shore of Old Tampa Bay, incorporated in 1917 and known locally as the home of the Espiritu Santo mineral springs. The entire city sits inside a single ZIP code, 34695, one of the more compact service areas in this part of Pinellas County. Safety Harbor's own water division maintains the local pipes and meters, but the city buys its treated drinking water wholesale from Pinellas County Utilities rather than pumping its own, and our emergency dispatch covers all of it around the clock.

Water Source & Housing

County Water, Established Streets

Because Safety Harbor buys treated water from Pinellas County's regional system, its water chemistry, including chloramine-based disinfection, matches Clearwater's rather than Dunedin's self-supplied wells. Housing in Safety Harbor is a mix of established mid-century streets near downtown and the waterfront, with Philippe Park's ancient Tocobaga mounds marking the city's oldest documented ground. Older galvanized supply lines still show up regularly in the neighborhoods closest to downtown.

A Note On The Springs

The Mineral Springs That Named The City

Safety Harbor's motto, Home of the Mineral Springs, refers to the Espiritu Santo springs that drew visitors for decades and eventually became the Safety Harbor Resort and Spa, a site the U.S. Department of the Interior designated a historical landmark in 1964. The springs are a natural mineral water source rather than the city's plumbing supply, but they're a genuine part of why Safety Harbor has stayed a small, tightly-bounded city instead of sprawling the way some Pinellas County neighbors have.
