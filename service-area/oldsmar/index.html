City Overview

Plumbing In Oldsmar, Florida

Oldsmar is a city of about 14,900 residents at the top of Tampa Bay, in the far northeast corner of Pinellas County. Automobile pioneer Ransom E. Olds bought roughly 37,500 acres here in 1916, and he pictured a city of 100,000 people. The real growth came much later, from 345 residents in 1950 to about 2,600 in 1980 and past 10,000 by the early 1990s. Our emergency dispatch covers all of Oldsmar around the clock.

Housing & Water

Newer Homes, A Mixed Water Supply

Because most of Oldsmar grew after 1980, many homes here are newer than the postwar housing in nearby cities, with copper or CPVC supply lines and PVC drains. The city treats brackish groundwater at its own reverse osmosis plant and has supplemented that supply with water bought from Pinellas County. That mix can taste and behave differently from the water in neighboring cities, so we test at your tap before recommending any treatment equipment.

Local History

A Founder's Dream, A Storm, And A New Name

The 1921 hurricane pushed water from Old Tampa Bay into Oldsmar, and Olds soon lost interest in his namesake town. The city was briefly renamed Tampashores before residents brought the Oldsmar name back. Today the Oldsmar Days and Nights festival celebrates that history with parades, carnival rides, and car shows that feature the Oldsmobile.

Neighborhoods & Landmarks

Areas We Cover In Oldsmar

We regularly work around State Road 580, Olds Park on the Tampa Bay waterfront, Veterans Memorial Park, and the Mobbly Bayou Wilderness Preserve. The city sits only a few feet above sea level, so low yards near the bay and the preserve are part of how we think about drainage. Neighborhoods on both sides of the main corridors fall inside the same dispatch zone.

Why Oldsmar Homes Call Us

Homes built from the late 1970s into the mid 1990s sometimes have polybutylene supply lines, which have a known history of failing without warning.

Low Elevation On The Bay

Oldsmar sits only a few feet above sea level at the top of Tampa Bay, so storm surge and heavy rain put real stress on drains and ground-level rooms.

Irrigation Leaks

Many Oldsmar yards have lawn irrigation, and a leaking underground zone line can run up a water bill with no sign inside the house.

Before We Arrive

If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak In Oldsmar

Shut Off The Main Valve

Look for the main shutoff at the water meter near the street or where the line enters the house, and close it before water spreads.

Kill Power To Wet Areas

Turn off the breaker for any outlet or appliance near standing water, and never touch a wet switch.

Clear The Immediate Area

Move rugs, furniture, and boxes away from the leak so they stay dry while help is on the way.

FAQs

Questions From Oldsmar

Does Oldsmar have its own water plant?

Yes. The city runs a reverse osmosis plant that treats brackish groundwater and can produce up to 2 million gallons a day, and it has also bought water from Pinellas County. Your bill names your utility, and we test at the tap before recommending treatment equipment.

Do you check for polybutylene pipe in older Oldsmar homes?

Yes. Homes built from the late 1970s into the mid 1990s sometimes have it, and we look for it during any supply-line repair so you can plan a replacement before it fails.

Is Oldsmar in Pinellas County or Hillsborough County?

Oldsmar is in Pinellas County, though it sits right at the Hillsborough County line and uses the 813 area code, so many people assume it belongs to Tampa. We dispatch from Clearwater and cover Oldsmar like any other Pinellas address.

How far is Oldsmar from your Clearwater base?

It is roughly 12 miles by road, mostly along State Road 580, and typically a 20 to 30 minute drive depending on traffic.

Do you cover every ZIP code in Oldsmar?

Yes, Oldsmar is served by the single 34677 ZIP code, and our dispatch covers all of it, from Olds Park on the bay to the neighborhoods along State Road 580.

Is emergency service available in Oldsmar?

Yes, our dispatch line is answered 24 hours a day, every day, for burst pipes, active leaks, and no-hot-water calls anywhere in Oldsmar.
