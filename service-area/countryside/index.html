The Area

Plumbing In Countryside

Countryside is a suburban part of north Clearwater, centered around U.S. Highway 19, Countryside Boulevard, Enterprise Road, and Curlew Road. Countryside Mall opened on September 8, 1975, and the name appears across the area, from Countryside High School and Countryside Country Club to a long list of subdivisions such as Clubhouse Estates of Countryside, Oak Forest of Countryside, and Westchester of Countryside.

Because Countryside is a collection of subdivisions and not one platted neighborhood, its edges are not sharply defined. Real estate listings place homes here in ZIP codes 33761 and 33763. If you are unsure whether your address counts as Countryside, call and we will confirm coverage.

Housing

Subdivisions From The 1970s And 1980s

The housing guides we reviewed describe Countryside homes from the 1970s and 1980s. Clubhouse Estates of Countryside shows single-family homes built from 1976 to 1982. The Countryside Northridge section has about 319 homes with a median year built of 1981, mostly ranch-style houses on larger than standard lots. The Trails of Countryside has about 379 homes with a median year built of 1970, plus some newer homes built after 2020. These are approximate figures from an aggregator.

Larger lots and newer additions can mean irrigation lines and outdoor hose connections that are easy to forget about. A small underground leak in a sprinkler line can run for a long time before anyone notices, so an unexplained water bill is worth a look.

Water Quality

Why The Water At Your Tap Can Differ

About 60 percent of Clearwater's drinking water comes from city-owned wells treated by reverse osmosis, and the rest is bought from Pinellas County Utilities. Which supply reaches a given address can affect how the water behaves at the tap, so we test at your faucet before suggesting any treatment equipment. Treatment equipment is only worth buying if your water calls for it.

FAQs

Questions From Countryside

What counts as Countryside?

Countryside is a group of subdivisions in north Clearwater around US-19 and Countryside Boulevard, and its edges are not sharply defined. Call with your address and we will confirm coverage.

Do you work on condos and townhomes in Countryside?

Yes. We service houses, condos, and townhomes. For a condo, tell us whether the problem may be in a shared line.

Should I get a water softener?

It depends on your water. We test at your tap first and recommend equipment only if the results support it.

How can I tell if I have an underground sprinkler leak?

An unexplained rise in your water bill, soggy patches in the yard, or low sprinkler pressure can all point to one. Call us and we will help you track it down.
