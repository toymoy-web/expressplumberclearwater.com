Water Heater Installation & Replacement

New Unit, Installed Same Day

Water Heater Installation & Replacement is one of the most-called services in Clearwater, and Gulf Coast hard water is a big part of why. Mineral buildup shortens tank life here faster than it does inland, so replacements come up more often than homeowners expect. We install both tank and tankless units and haul away the old one at no extra charge.

Why This Happens Here

Hard Water Shortens Tank Life

Scale from Gulf Coast hard water settles at the bottom of a tank and coats the heating element, forcing the unit to work harder to reach the same temperature. Over years, that buildup is the single biggest reason tank water heaters fail early here compared to homes on softer municipal water elsewhere in the country. A softener slows this process considerably, which is why we often recommend pairing a new heater with one if the home doesn't already have a working system.

Common Problems

Signs You Need a Replacement, Not a Repair

No Hot Water

Cold showers, or a tank that can't keep up with normal household use.

Rusty Water

Discolored hot water usually means the tank itself is corroding from the inside.

Popping Or Rumbling Noises

Sediment buildup on the tank floor rumbling as the unit heats.

Age Over 10 Years

Most tank units are on borrowed time once they pass a decade, especially here.

No Hot Water Right Now?

A technician can install a new unit today in most cases.

Tank vs. Tankless

Which Fits Your Home

Traditional Tank

  • Lower upfront cost
  • Simpler installation
  • Limited hot water supply
  • Typically 10-15 year lifespan

Tankless

  • Endless hot water on demand
  • Lower monthly energy cost
  • Compact wall-mounted unit
  • Typically 20+ year lifespan
